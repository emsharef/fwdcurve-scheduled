# Licensing and source attribution

The root MIT license covers the original Python software, Lean source, and
repository guides. It does not relicense third-party dependencies, market data,
source documents, or the manuscripts and their figures. The manuscripts are
provided for reading and scholarly citation; no separate reuse license is
granted here for their text and figures.

The empirical inputs contain observations derived from CME bulletins, Eris
valuation curves, and Federal Reserve calendars. Their providers retain their
respective rights. Public availability is not an MIT license for those data.
See the [data guide](docs/data.md) for provenance and the distinction between
prepared inputs and original source downloads. Third-party research articles
and the local lab's full-text literature collection are not distributed.

Lean and Mathlib dependencies are obtained separately at the revisions in
`lean/lake-manifest.json`; their licenses remain applicable. Literature
attributions in Lean comments and manuscript bibliographies are retained.
