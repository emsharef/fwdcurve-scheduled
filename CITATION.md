# Citing this work

For a mathematical result, cite the corresponding manuscript by its title,
version date, and numbered result. The [proof map](docs/proof-map.md) gives the
corresponding original result and Lean declaration. Use the authors as listed
on the manuscript version you cite; the present drafts do not supply an author
line or a journal DOI.

For calculations or proof artifacts, also record the repository commit:

```bibtex
@misc{fwdcurve_scheduled_software,
  title = {Forward curves with scheduled dates: software and formal proofs},
  year = {2026},
  howpublished = {\url{https://github.com/emsharef/fwdcurve-scheduled}},
  note = {Repository commit: INSERT-COMMIT; accessed: INSERT-DATE}
}
```

The two manuscript titles are:

- *Forward curves with scheduled dates: consistency, realizations, and correlation*.
- *What options reveal about policy-meeting risk: identification and factor-model restrictions*.

Bibliographic keys in the source comments refer to the manuscript `.bib` files
or the archived [reference registry](docs/archive/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md). The registry
is provenance, not a claim that every listed work is cited in both manuscripts.
